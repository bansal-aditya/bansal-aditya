### 👋 Hi, I'm Aditya

I am a Software Engineer, AI/ML at **Google** working on the **Gemini Agent Platform**. Prior to this, I was a Senior ML Engineer at **Adobe** building Enterprise AI Agents. 

I completed my Master's at **Carnegie Mellon University's** Language Technologies Institute (LTI) and my B.Eng. in Computer Science with a specialization in AI from **Nanyang Technological University (NTU)**. My research and engineering focus centers on **agentic systems**, **LLM evaluation & post-training**, **synthetic data generation**, and **neural network compression**.
